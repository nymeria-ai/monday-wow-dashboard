How this was built

  • Google: accounts Main 3746504118 + Locals 9441310809 (all campaigns), payers = conversion action "Paying (MCC)", all conversions, by conversion date. Customer totals checked against the campaign sums: match.
  • Bing: accounts Big 4 135096643 + Locals 135096648, PMAX excluded. Payers = "paying" goal (campaign level, 31017633 / 31018718) and "paying - account" (31008495), taking the higher one per campaign-day so nothing is counted twice. Spend matches the Microsoft Ads UI/API to the cent (June checked).
  • ⚠️ Bing tracking gap Jun 21 – Jul 9: all Bing signup goals drop ~95% while spend kept running, and paying shows 0 for Jun 17–30. Bing payers and CAC for June and July are not reliable (June CAC $24.9K is mostly the gap).
  • Bing payer counts are small (5–28 a week), so weekly Bing CAC moves a lot. The monthly view is the better read.
  • Payers come in late (about 84% within 30 days). Every week here is 30+ days old, so the numbers are close to final, Oct can still grow a little.
  • Google Aug 5–11 (214 payers) is a known outlier, about 2x a normal week.
