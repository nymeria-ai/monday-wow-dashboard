How this was built

  • Google, Platform: accounts Main 3746504118 + Locals 9441310809, all campaigns (all Search, no PMAX; includes ~$1.8M of CRM campaigns in Locals). Spend + conversion action "Paying (MCC)", all conversions, credited to the click date (same numbers as the earlier version of this page).
  • Bing, Platform: Big 4 135096643 + Locals 135096648, PMAX campaigns excluded (~$0.14M). The two paying goals are never added together. Per account, from the first day "paying - account" (31008495) recorded a conversion, only "paying - account" counts; before that day only the old "paying" goal (31017633 / 31018718) counts. Cutover: Big 4 Jul 1 2026, Locals Aug 9 2026 (checked on daily data).
  • ⚠️ Bing tracking gap Jun 21 – ~Aug 1 2026: Bing signup and paying goals almost stopped counting while spend kept running (Locals recorded nothing at all). Platform Bing payers and CAC for Jun–Aug 2026 are not reliable. No other goal gap found in Jun 2025 – May 2026: weekly goals are continuous; the only dips (Christmas weeks, late Nov) follow spend. Old "hard signup" jumps ~40% from mid-Aug 2025 while work signups stay flat (a signup-goal change, does not touch payers).
  • BigBrain: bigbrain.l3.fact_campaign_monitoring_dwh. Spend = ad network cost by day. Payers = eligible accounts (no test, monday-domain or ineligible) with a first payment date, counted in their signup week/month. Bing = sources bing + binglocals (non-brand, bingbrand excluded, PMAX excluded). Google = sources adwordssearch + adwordslocals, limited to the Main + Locals accounts through the campaign→account map in fact_marketing_adn_spend_daily (Verticals, Verticals2, brand account, YouTube/DemandGen excluded).
  • Timing: Platform credits a payer to the click week; BigBrain to the signup week. Click → signup is the same week 99.97% of the time, so the two line up. Payment comes later: 44% of payers pay 14–30 days after signup and ~7% after 90 days, so Aug–Oct 2026 payers (both sources) will still grow.
  • Google Aug 5–11 2026 (214 platform payers) is a known outlier, about 2x a normal week.
  • Bing payer counts are small (often under 20 a week), so weekly Bing CAC is noisy. Read the monthly view.
  • First chart week (May 28 – Jun 3 2025): Platform covers Jun 1–3 only, BigBrain the full week. Last week (Oct 14–15) is partial for both.
  • Scope match, Platform vs BigBrain: spend lines up within ~0.1% for both channels after two fixes. (1) Google: the BigBrain source adwordssearch also holds the Verticals/Verticals2 accounts (~$28.8M), mapped out through campaign→account. 26K signups / 187 payers with a Google campaign name that couldn't be mapped to an account were left out of BigBrain, so BigBrain Google payers are slightly low. (2) Bing: the BigBrain source "bing" also picked up the monday CRM Microsoft Ads account (135108872, ~$162K, Sep 14 – Oct 15 2026). It isn't in the platform pull, so it was removed from BigBrain (7 payers).
  • Bing BigBrain spend for Aug/Sep 2025 and Jan/Feb 2026 is a few $K below the platform (max $3.6K a month). BigBrain CAC uses BigBrain spend.
