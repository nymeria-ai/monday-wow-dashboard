How the recommendation works: every cluster starts from its current share of spend. That share is then multiplied by (average CAC ÷ the cluster's smoothed CAC). Smoothing adds 10 "virtual" payers at the period's average CAC, so a cluster with only a few payers can't look great or terrible by luck. Each cluster can move at most ×0.5 to ×1.5 from its current share, to protect against diminishing returns at higher bids and against learning-phase resets. Then everything is normalized to 100%. The recommendation is calculated separately for each period tab. Upside assumes CAC holds, which is an upper bound.
Sources: Google uses "Paying (MCC)" all_conversions. Bing uses the "paying - account" + "paying" goals (all 3 accounts). These are platform-attributed numbers, not BI. The cluster mapping is the same as the WoW dashboard (crm/service/globster/elevate/taka excluded).
