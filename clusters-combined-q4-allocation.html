How the recommendation works: every cluster starts from its current share of spend. That share is then multiplied by (average CAC ÷ the cluster's smoothed CAC). Smoothing adds 10 "virtual" payers at the average CAC ($7.3K), so a cluster with only a few payers can't look great or terrible by luck. Each cluster can move at most ×0.5 to ×1.5 from its current share, to protect against diminishing returns at higher bids and against learning-phase resets. Then everything is normalized to 100%. At the same total budget and the same CAC per cluster, this mix works out to about 3,210 payers instead of 2,952 (+9%). That's an upper bound: CAC usually gets worse as spend goes up.
